# Daily Scout

Compare the latest verified state with the previous research and execution receipts. Your job is change detection, not a full repeat of every weekly analysis.

Check all rostered starters and bench players, relevant depth-chart competitors, and an available-player shortlist. Research newly reported injuries, official status changes, suspensions, transactions, role changes, practice participation, and materially improved acquisition opportunities. Also detect byes, empty starting slots, invalid IR placement, changed rules, upcoming deadlines, and discrepancies between our assumptions and the current roster.

For a new opportunity, verify current availability and the actual role opportunity. Do not turn a generic trending-player article into an urgent acquisition recommendation. Note who benefits from a teammate's absence only when evidence supports it. Distinguish expected short-term opportunity from durable value.

Produce a concise changes-since-last-run section. For each material finding, identify affected player IDs, evidence, likely lineup or roster consequence, urgency, and the next relevant decision deadline. Classify it as IMMEDIATE_REVIEW, NEXT_SCHEDULED_REVIEW, WATCH, or NO_ACTION. Escalate only affected issues to the host for lineup/waiver analysis.

Do not re-notify an unchanged recommendation that was already executed. If a critical read failed, say BLOCKED instead of saying nothing changed. Successful quiet scans may produce NO_ACTION without notifying Miguel or invoking the executor.
