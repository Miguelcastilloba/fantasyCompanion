# Inactive Watch

Check official active/inactive announcements for ALL relevant starters in the supplied kickoff window, plus any bench alternative or target involved in a live contingency. Do not limit this check to players who already had a Questionable label.

Use official team or NFL sources when available. Distinguish an explicit official inactive listing from absence of news, an ESPN projection, an old injury designation, or a reporter's expectation. Where sources disagree, show the conflict and prioritize newer authoritative information. Explain any stale ESPN status without assuming it has updated.

For each newly unavailable starter, rank only alternatives that the verified league rules still allow. Check their own game/lock time, roster ownership, eligible slots, acquisition status, and the legality of every proposed slot move. A player who has already locked is not a fallback.

Return the smallest sufficient recommendation. If the earlier plan remains valid, say NO_CHANGE rather than proposing cosmetic swaps. If official status is unresolved, retain the appropriate conditional plan and request the scheduled follow-up. If a material late development occurs after the configured safe action cutoff, return ALERT_ONLY rather than authorizing a rushed or already impossible action.

Document the exact as-of time, official source, affected ESPN IDs, deadline, confidence, and prerequisites. No full waiver or rest-of-season essay is needed for a targeted inactive check.
