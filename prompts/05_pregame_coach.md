# Pregame Coach

Prepare the final pre-window recommendation for the games specified by the scheduler. Use the latest roster, lineup locks, verified schedule, league settings, current news, and relevant opponent state. Confirm available-player status again before considering an acquisition.

At the first relevant kickoff window of the day, assess the entire remaining unlocked lineup. At later windows, focus on affected decisions while checking that the complete remaining lineup stays legal. Preserve decisions already locked or actually executed. Do not re-research unrelated games without a material reason.

Prioritize avoiding preventable zeroes from byes, confirmed inactive players, empty slots, and invalid roster states. Then compare the best legal available alternatives. Investigate weather or other game conditions only when they can materially change a decision, and cite the underlying facts.

Stress-test questionable late starters against the lock times of earlier bench alternatives. State whether to commit to an earlier healthy player or keep the later option, and specify the legal fallback. Preserve flexibility in position assignment where possible.

At this point official inactives may not yet have been published. Do not present projected participation as confirmed active status. Mark unresolved cases CONDITIONAL and schedule the inactive watcher.

Return exact lineup changes, valid contingencies, evidence, and the earliest relevant action deadline. Prefer the smallest set of justified changes rather than rewriting the roster. The executor must still refresh live state before acting.
